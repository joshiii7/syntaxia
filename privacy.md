## Information I collect

None. Syntaxia has no accounts, no sign up, and no forms, so there is nothing for you to type in and nothing for me to receive.

## What's saved in your browser

To remember where you are, Syntaxia saves a few small things in your own browser's local storage: which lessons you've completed, which learning path you've picked, and whether you prefer the light or dark theme. This stays on your device. It's never sent to me or anyone else, and you can erase it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

## Code you write

The code you type into a lesson's editor runs right in your browser. It isn't saved or sent anywhere, and it's gone when you leave or reload the page.

## Cookies and tracking

Syntaxia doesn't use cookies, analytics, ads, or any tracking scripts.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record basic technical details, such as your IP address, when your browser loads a page. That's handled under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), not by me, and I don't have access to it.

## Other sites

A few lesson examples load a web font from Google Fonts when their preview runs, which means your browser asks Google for that font. Links to other sites, like GitHub or my portfolio, take you somewhere with its own privacy policy.

## Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, for example if the site ever adds accounts, this page will be updated first, along with the date at the top.
